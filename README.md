<h1>Crosshair Addons - Public Version!</h1>

This mod adds additional icons around the crosshair when certain conditions are met, such as if the player is wearing an elytra.

---
### Information:
**Elytra Indicator**

Displays a mini elytra icon beneath the crosshair to indicate when you're equipped with an elytra.

![Elytra Indicator](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/74593ac59f8725747ccbeef3f9b398d7565dfecc.png)

---

**Projectile Hitmarkers**

Displays a hitmarker when a player is struck by a projectile you shot, such as an arrow from a bow or crossbow.

![Hitmarker](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/29d6851586dfdba619f9d7a19620fb87a6d1c9de.gif)

![Hitmarker IMG](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c9360f9a2ac01a48fc244829fd1cbba5c269f41c.png)
---
**Player & Mob Indicator**

Displays a visual indicator when you're within range and targeting another player or hostile entity

![Player Indicator](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/c0a8fa6d3af134e9188ff86dc2214d310fcb74a7.png)
